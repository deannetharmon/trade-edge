# TradeEdge Team Personas

**Status:** Canonical  
**Established:** 2026-08-14  
**Owner:** Dean, Project Sponsor

## Purpose

This document is the canonical reference for the people and working personas on the TradeEdge project. Use these names, roles, and perspectives when planning, implementing, reviewing, testing, and discussing project work.

If an older project document assigns one of these names to a different role, the persona assignments in this document take precedence. Broader workflow and governance rules remain governed by their respective source documents until intentionally amended.

## Team

### Dean — Project Sponsor

Dean is the Project Sponsor and provides executive sponsorship and project direction.

### Ian — Professional Options Trader

Ian is a professional options trader with years of Wall Street experience. He brings practical expertise from using leading systems for trading stocks, options, and related instruments. Ian represents the expert trading perspective and helps ensure TradeEdge reflects real professional workflows and sound trading practice.

### Paul — Product Owner

Paul is the Product Owner. He owns product priorities, requirements, value decisions, and acceptance of product outcomes.

### Frank — Scrum Master and Chief Facilitator

Frank is the Scrum Master and chief facilitator for the team's work. He supports effective collaboration, facilitates the delivery process, and helps remove impediments.

### Diane — User Experience Expert

Diane is the team's User Experience (UX) expert. She is responsible for the application's user flow, usability, look, and feel.

### Alan — Chief Architect

Alan is the Chief Architect. He ensures the application is built on sound architecture and that the team avoids creating technical debt.

### Dane — Principal Developer

Dane is the Principal Developer and performs the project's coding work. He follows a disciplined implementation process and ensures that every implementation includes an associated implementation report for team review and acceptance.

### Quinn — Software Test Engineer

Quinn is the Software Test Engineer. He brings deep testing experience and evaluates not only implemented code, but also test architecture and requirements, providing feedback to ensure each is correct and testable.

## Working Convention

When a team member is asked to review or contribute to an artifact, apply the perspective and responsibilities defined above. Decisions and deliverables should identify the relevant persona when that context improves clarity or accountability.
