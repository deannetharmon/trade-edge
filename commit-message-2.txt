its top maxQualifiedPairsRetained/maxNearMissPairsRetained pairs

survive, not the individual pairs. A real, valid, qualified structure
someone is comparing against their own manually-built broker trade
can be entirely invisible in the retained set, with no way to tell
"genuinely disqualified" from "truncated by the retention limit."

  gates the full scan uses, applied to one specific pair. Five
  outcomes: not_found_in_chain, leg_rejected, pair_rejected,
  qualified, near_miss.
- Always evaluates against the criteria passed in, never a cached
  value -- verified by test.
- The motivating test builds 12 genuinely qualified pairs, confirms
  the full scan's retention limit truncates one out of view, then
  proves the on-demand check correctly identifies that truncated
  pair as real and qualified.
