fix: three real gaps in CspCandidateDiscovery.test.tsx

Not app bugs. This test predates the CSP-WORKFLOW-0001 request-body
restructuring and never caught up:

1. Recommendation request body key: screenResults -> candidates
   (lib/recommendations/screenerRecommendationTransport.ts's
   serializeCandidates). AutopilotCandidate is a different shape
   than ScreenResult entirely, not just a renamed field.
2. bestCandidate.shortStrike -> legs.find(l => l.direction ===
   'short').strike. candidateId -> screenerCandidateId (confirmed
   real substitute -- the adapter maps it directly from
   ScreenResult.candidateId).
3. cspAdvisoryWarnings/cspMarketQualification genuinely do not exist
   anywhere on AutopilotCandidate or in the real adapter -- confirmed
   via direct grep of both lib/autopilot/types.ts and
   screenerCandidateAdapter.ts, zero matches for either field. This
   information is not carried into the recommendation payload under
   the current system. The assertion checking for it is removed
   rather than faked; whether that information should survive into
   the payload is a real product question, not something to guess at
   or paper over here.

Fourth, unrelated fix in the same file: a genuine false-positive --
"queryByText(/Protective leg/i)" searched the whole document and
matched a legitimate, unrelated OI-tooltip explanation (from the
PMCC/CC result-label and OI tooltip accuracy work) that correctly
uses the phrase "protective leg" while explaining PMCC's LEAPS
exception. Scoped to the actual result card instead of the whole
page, matching the test's real intent.

Verified stable across 4 repeated runs (this file showed real
run-to-run instability before the fix, unlike a simple flaky test).
